Privacy Policy – Trump the World
Last updated: April 22, 2026
1. Overview
Trump the World respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile game available on the Google Play Store.
2. No Data Collection
We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from users.
This includes, but is not limited to:
•	No names, email addresses, or contact information
•	No location data
•	No device identifiers used for tracking
•	No gameplay data linked to individuals
3. No Data Use
Because we do not collect any data:
•	We do not analyze user behavior
•	We do not profile users
•	We do not use data for advertising or marketing
4. No Data Sharing
We do not share any user data with third parties, because we do not collect any data in the first place.
5. Third-Party Services
Trump the World does not use third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies that collect personal information.
However, please note that the Google Play Store itself may collect certain information as part of its standard operations. We do not control this data collection.
6. Children’s Privacy
Our game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (United States) or under applicable provincial age thresholds in Canada.
7. Security
Since we do not collect or store any user data, there is no risk of personal data breaches originating from our game.
8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy if the game’s functionality changes (for example, if data collection features are introduced).
If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, users will be clearly notified and will be required to provide explicit consent to the updated policy before continuing to use the game.
